Alarm Klock Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

What we collect

Alarm Klock does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address or location. Your alarms and settings are stored on your device only.

Camera and motion

The camera is used only during Photo and QR missions, and photos are processed on your device and are not saved or uploaded. Motion sensors are used only during Shake, Tilt and Squat missions and the data stays on your device.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipt and subscription status. It does not receive your name or contact details from us. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Tracking and advertising

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites and we show no ads.

Children

Alarm Klock is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

You can delete all app data by deleting the app. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings.

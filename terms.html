Alarm Klock Terms of Use

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Subscription

Alarm Klock Premium is an auto-renewing subscription: $2.99 per month or $19.99 per year (prices may vary by country). New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase, or at the end of the free trial.

Renewal and cancellation

Your subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel your subscription any time in your Apple ID account settings after purchase. Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription.

Alarm reliability

Alarm Klock is provided "as is". Keep your device charged, your volume up and notifications allowed. Do not rely on any single alarm for critical events.

# Alarm Klock privacy policy
Alarm Klock stores your alarms on your device only. We collect no account data. The camera is used only during Photo and QR missions and photos are not saved or uploaded. Motion sensors are used only during Shake, Tilt and Squat missions. Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google and RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase status. Invite friends: to create your invite code and give the free month of Pro, our server stores the same anonymous app user ID, your invite code and whether a code was redeemed. No name, email, phone number or contacts are collected; the invite message is sent by you from your own apps. Contact: samayhuf@gmail.com
